How BundleBaskets Works

From service bundle to beautiful basket in three steps.

1

Bundle with a Partner

Select longer contracts or multi-service packages with participating banking, telecom, home security, medical, or liquor providers. Deposit tiers and multi-year commitments unlock higher baskets.

2

Choose or Build Your Basket

Build a virtual basket ($19.95–$195 for liquor; $25–$150 for others). Instantly see qualifying bundles. If the basket exceeds the reward, pay the balance in cash. Age verification required by state law for alcohol.

3

Receive Your Reward

The provider funds the basket as a loyalty thank-you. Longer commitments unlock higher tiers. Businesses can fund larger custom rewards.

Compliance-first design: Baskets are structured as post-purchase loyalty rewards. Providers control eligibility and funding. Liquor baskets require age verification per state law. Users may pay any balance in cash.
